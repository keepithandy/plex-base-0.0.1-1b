# P2-11 balanced-wording candidate

Status: **review-only candidate prepared; no training approval exists and no run has started.**

## Why this candidate

P2-10's frozen checkpoint scored 43/64 on four additional held-out phrasings. Its read-only token audit found two distinct weak conditions: “Reproduce the selector below verbatim” scored 6/16, with answer-start skips on next-line inputs and duplicated newlines; “Write this selector again, character for character” scored 5/16, with selector-body divergence often favoring `charlie-list`. “Transcribe” and “Preserve” each scored 16/16. The expected period after the supplied newline and EOS after the full expected answer were top-ranked throughout, pointing to phrasing-conditioned start/body selection in this narrow task.

P2-11 asks whether balanced exposure to those two weak phrasings improves selector copying on fresh wording. It is a diagnostic, not a controlled causal comparison: the additions also increase the number of unique training records.

## Candidate matrix

- Training: **96 records**, six phrasings × two colon layouts × eight known selectors. The four original P2-10 training phrasings are retained; the two weak P2-10 wider-evaluation phrasings are added.
- Evaluation-only: **64 records**, four entirely new phrasings × two layouts × the same eight selectors.
- The exact 32 requests from the two weak P2-10 evaluation phrasings are intentionally promoted to P2-11 training. Their old scores remain valid historical P2-10 results, but those requests are no longer a clean holdout for a future run.
- All 64 fresh evaluation requests are disjoint from P2-10 training, P2-10 original evaluation, P2-10 wider evaluation, and P2-11 training. Evaluation rows are marked evaluation-only and may not enter training or runtime validation loss.
- Every record retains P2-10's explicit answer-start contract and exact selector-copy target. The tokenizer is frozen; model architecture, parameter count, selector inventory, layouts, and answer-focused objective are unchanged.
